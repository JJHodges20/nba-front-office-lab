# What a Player Is Worth

The player value model: what it estimates, how it was tested, and what it found. Generated by `fol value report --write` from revision 1 of the fitted model (fitted 2026-10-02) and build 13; do not edit it by hand - fit the model, rebuild, and run the command again.

> Everything here is an estimate. Impact is what a model fitted to thirty seasons of team results makes of a player's statistics; it is tested on seasons the model had not seen, and it is wrong by a known amount. It is not a measurement, and no weight in it is the price of anything.

## What is estimated, and how

A player's **impact** is the points per 100 possessions a team's net rating gains with the player on the court, against the league's average minute. It is the sum of an offensive and a defensive estimate.

A team's rating in a season is modelled as the sum of its players' estimates, each counted for the share of the minutes the player actually played, where every estimate is built **only from seasons before the one predicted**. The weights are the ones that make that sum come closest to what teams then did. They are chosen for one thing: describing a player in a way that carries over to next season, on whatever team.

- Fitted on 863 team-seasons and 14,568 player-seasons, 1996-97 to 2025-26.
- Earlier seasons are pooled by minutes, newest first, at weights 1, 0.5, 0.25.
- A player's own numbers get half the say at 750 weighted minutes; the rest is what is assumed of any player used that many minutes a game.
- The reasoning behind each choice, and what was tried and left out: `docs/decisions/0010-player-value.md`.

## From impact to wins

- **Replacement level is -2.32.** Measured, not assumed: the minutes-weighted impact of players outside their team's 10 most-used, over every fitted season.
- **A point of net rating has been worth 2.23 to 2.63 wins over 82 games**, fitted for each season on that season and 2 either side: 2.57 in 1996-97, 2.23 in 2025-26.
- **Wins above replacement** are impact over replacement level, times the share of the team's minutes played, times that season's wins per point.
- **Impacts are cautious.** A team's net rating is 1.23 times the sum of its players' impacts in the same season (correlation 0.96). Impact is an estimate of ability, not a split of results, and nothing forces the two to agree.

## Projected minutes

Next season's share of team minutes, from the last three seasons' shares and age, fitted on every player seen in those three seasons - including the ones who then did not play at all.

- Tested on 13,675 player-seasons: RMSE 0.130 of a team's minutes, against 0.143 for assuming last season's share (-0.012, -0.013 to -0.011).
- Coefficients: intercept +0.017, share_1 +0.704, share_2 +0.111, share_3 +0.029, years_over_30 -0.008, years_under_25 +0.026.
- It knows nothing about injuries, trades, contracts or depth charts.

## The roles

Archetypes are rules, not findings (`configs/archetypes.yaml`, version 1): a player's score for a role is the weakest of their percentiles on the role's measures, among the players the role is open to, in that season, and a player fills the role from its cut-off up. Nothing here is fitted. What can be measured is whether a role is a lasting trait: of the players who filled it in one season, the share who filled it again in the next, against the share of everyone scored for it who did.

## What it cannot tell you

- **Defense is the weak side.** A box score records little of it, so the model leans on the team's own defensive results, shared among the players who were there. A good defender on a poor defensive team is underrated, and the reverse.
- **The test takes next season's minutes as given.** It shows that the ratings carry over, not that the lab can foresee who will play.
- **A season is described by its statistics, not by how it was achieved.** Role, scheme and teammates shape the numbers a player puts up, and the model sees only the numbers.
- **Playoff performance is not in it.** Counting playoff minutes did not help predict the next season, so they are left out; whether they matter in the playoffs is a different question.
- **It must be fitted again when a season ends.** This revision knows seasons to 2025-26.
